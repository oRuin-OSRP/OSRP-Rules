Welcome to OldSchool Roleplay

A real tight-knit community focused on bringing back the old school era of video gaming.

General Rules

NLR applies here, do not return to your place of death for at least 3 minutes. You forget everything that happened in the previous life.
Acting on and or relaying information in OOC is metagame, this is not allowed.
Roleplay related information must be posted in /advert chat and will be considered metagaming if posted in OOC chat(s).
English only. (You can speak any language via PM or in private areas)
Offensive/sexual sprays are not allowed.
Don't build on any public area. (Hobos are an exception, they can build in any place an admin deems reasonable)
Random Death Match (RDM) is NOT allowed.
Prop-abusing is NOT allowed.
Inappropriate or offensive prop constructions are not allowed.
You can kill players who insult you after you have warned them to stop(do not use mic to warn).
Do not block off public areas, roads, streets, etc.
Heavy rages / Deep insults will not be tolerated.
Any sort of hacking/cheating isn't allowed.
Advertising any community/forum is not allowed.
Threatening to attack the server in any manner will result in a permanent ban. Even if it was meant as a joke.
Do not place random radios or televisions around the map, and do not run around the map with them on.
Bug abuse of any sort is not allowed.

Car Rules

If someone steals your car you are allowed to get it back.
If someone is on your car you may shoot them after giving them a warning
Do not block public places or bases with cars. Do not attempt to drive cars inside buildings not made for cars.

Raiding Rules

Do not minge raid - Raiding a base you know has absolutely nothing in it or raiding an unowned base just to shoot people.
Props are not a reason to raid or kill.
The police can raid to confiscate printers, drugs and weapons but only with a valid warrant. They cannot steal legal items - Health, armor and money.
After being raided, you must wait 3 minutes before returning to your home (NLR)
You must wait 5 minutes after raiding, before raiding the same person again.
Thieves, Parkours & Gangsters(2 or more) are the only jobs allowed to raid. Hitmen may raid to take out their hit.
Gangsters & Mob Boss together are able to raid the PD to release gang members from prison.
The only other job allowed to raid the PD are terrorists with Osama.
You CAN be raided while building. Create an entrance by placing a keypad & fading door before building anything else!
You may kill someone in order to steal something that they are holding or if a car owner is standing next to their vehicle.
You do not need to /advert raid or mug.
If another player is in the process of lockpicking a vehicle that you desire, you may kill them in order to steal it yourself.
The Radio host's microphone is not a reason to raid and you are not allowed to steal it.

Shop, Bases & Home Rules

You may only have THREE fading doors for the entrance of your base. You are allowed to have more fading doors to cover valuables in eg. a container, as long as they do not require keypad cracking.
There must be AT LEAST one entrance to your shop/base.
An entrance needs to be clearly indicated.
Do not make an entrance that requires crouching.
Your keypad granted length must be set so you have enough time to be able to get inside. (Minimum 3 seconds)
You cannot put keypads or buttons far away from your entrance, or make them hidden. They must be clearly visible near your door.
Do not build a base specifically made to trap people (even if it has a keypad to get out).
There must be a keypad or button linked to your fading door allowing players to enter and leave.
Using a toggled fading door does not allow you to use only one button or keypad.
You can have a maximum of one keypad or one button (each way) per fading door.
Using the color/material tool to create invisible or barely visible props/buttons/cameras etc. is not allowed.
Using fake keypads, buttons or a fall hatch/trap is NOT allowed.
Making or owning a base with a job of the opposite class is NOT allowed. Example: Cops or Mayor basing with criminals.
A keypad and/or button is required on the inside of any enclosed buying area.
The official owner of a house is the player who owns the front door.
Do not buy random houses and doors that you aren't going to use.
Do not charge players money for houses you aren't using (You can charge money if someone would like to purchase a place that you are currently using.)
Government Officials is not allowed to base with criminals or terrorist and vice versa (Being a corrupt government official is no reason to break this rule).
KOS lines/zones are NOT allowed. The only time 'KOS' can be used is if the player has entered your property.
Hit-box abuse is not allowed. Example: Creating a base that allows you to shoot any raider but the raider cannot shoot you back.
Fading door abuse is not allowed. Opening a fading door using the bound key is considered fading door abuse - (Use your keypad/button!)
You must warn players to leave your base before killing them unless you have a KOS sign warning them.
Making a base C4-proof or battering ram-proof is not allowed.

General Job Rules

No class/job can take hostages with the exception of Osama who may take the Mayor hostage.
Do not job abuse. Example: Dropping the weapon(s) that you spawned with as a specific job or purchasing weapons for yourself to use as another job.
Thieves and hobos are allowed to pickpocket afk users but may still be arrested for doing so.
You are only allowed to sell weapons as a weapon dealing job. ( Gun dealer, BMD, Elite Seller and any custom dealer job)
The only job that can mug other players is two gangsters or more.
Any interaction between you and any player is forgotten / forgiven once you are out of prison.
Do not randomly stunstick players.
Only Gangsters, Cops and Terrorists can defend each other in the street.
Using cameras for other reasons than surveillance of your own base is not allowed.
By default all weapons are legal with a license, unless the laws state otherwise.
Any public homes made with props is considered to be "Inside" during a lockdown.
Advertising that you are doing something illegal is a reason for cops to warrant/wanted you.
WARS are not allowed (e.g. hobos vs cops, gang A vs gang B)
As a parkour, do not place printers where other jobs can't reach.
